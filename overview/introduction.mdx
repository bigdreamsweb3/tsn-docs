Transfer Settlement Network (TSN) is an identity-first, intent-based, privacy-preserving settlement layer for stablecoin and digital asset transfers, built by [TrustLink Labs](/about/trustlink-labs) on Solana. TSN separates _who authorizes a transfer_, _who routes the work_, and _who submits the transaction_, so operators can move traffic through the system without being able to change what the sender approved.

## What TSN gives you

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Identity-aware authorization" icon="fingerprint">
    Payments are authorized by a Transfer Identity Number (TIN) and routed through GPRU, not by a bare public key. Sender terms are signed and bound to the intent.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Treasury-backed settlement" icon="vault">
    The Epoch Treasury holds funds and tracks liability against Mother authority. Every settlement is checked against stored state and signed fields before it commits.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Confidential balance accounting" icon="lock">
    TCAP records private balance transitions as commitments and encrypted snapshots. Owners can read their own balance; the ledger stores only the commitment.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The five roles

TSN runs on Solana today. Its architecture splits the payment lifecycle into five roles with distinct trust:

| Role | Owns | Cannot do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sender \+ TIN** | Signs the payment intent | — |
| **GPRU routing** | Resolves route metadata privately | Change intent fields |
| **Mother authority** | Governs settlement authorization | Rewrite sender-signed terms |
| **Node** | Verifies signatures, sequence, expiry, policy | Sign as Mother or submit transactions |
| **Receiver \+ Cranker** | Stores work and submits authorized transactions on Solana | Change recipient, mint, amount, nonce, nullifier, or DNA |

The defining property: **transport does not authorize, and authorization does not require transport to be honest.** See [Separation of concerns](/overview/separation-of-concerns) for the full boundary.

## The payment path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sender signs a payment intent">
    A signed intent binds recipient, amount, mint, nonce, and validity window to the sender's TIN. The intent is off-chain and never public.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Node verifies">
    A stateless Node verifies signatures, TIN and route bindings, expiry, sequence, and policy checks. The Node has no signing authority.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receiver stores and leases the work">
    The Receiver is durable infrastructure for verified work. It leases jobs to Crankers under a short lease and enforces state versions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cranker submits on Solana">
    The Cranker pays fees and submits the exact authorized transaction. The Solana program checks stored state, signed fields, and nullifiers before it commits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Epoch Treasury settles; TCAP records the delta">
    The Epoch Treasury moves funds and records liability. TCAP writes a commitment and encrypted snapshot so the owner can reconcile their private balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Core primitives

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="TIN and GPRU: identity and routing" icon="id-card">
    A **Transfer Identity Number (TIN)** is a payment identity resolved privately. **GPRU** routes work between endpoints without taking custody of funds. Routing metadata stays off-chain; the chain sees only commitments and derived PDAs. See [Identity and routing](/architecture/identity-and-routing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Mother authority" icon="shield-halved">
    Mother is the root TSN authority and epoch controller: a Program-Derived Address whose stored `authority` is a governed external keypair. Mother authorizes intent acceptance and settlement DNA. See [Mother authority](/architecture/mother-authority).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Settlement DNA, nullifiers, and sequence" icon="dna">
    Settlement DNA binds payout parameters (recipient, amount, mint, nonce, digest, expiry) to a slot. Nullifiers, receipts, sequence checks, and validity windows enforce one-time state consumption. See [Replay protection](/security/replay-protection).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Epoch Treasury" icon="vault">
    The Epoch Treasury holds epoch-level source liquidity and liabilities. Treasury and vault accounting separate funds from pending obligations. See [Epoch Treasury](/architecture/epoch-treasury).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="TCAP commitments" icon="lock">
    TCAP records private balance transitions as commitments and owner-decryptable encrypted snapshots. See [TCAP](/architecture/tcap).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
