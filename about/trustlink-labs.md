# TrustLink Labs

TrustLink Labs is the team building the Transfer Settlement Network (TSN), a Solana-first payment-settlement system for identity-aware authorization, treasury-backed settlement, and confidential balance accounting.

The team focuses on making payment infrastructure verifiable at every boundary. TSN separates the sender's authorization from routing and transaction submission, while TCAP records private balance transitions as commitments and encrypted snapshots.

## What TrustLink Labs is building

- Identity-aware payment routing through TINs and GPRU.
- Explicit authorization and settlement liability on Solana.
- Operational tooling for Receivers, Nodes, Crankers, Mother authority, and governance.
- Confidential accounting through TCAP commitments and encrypted owner-readable state.
- Developer documentation that describes the current implementation accurately.

These docs describe the technology that exists in the current TSN system. Future chain adapters and research directions are not presented as live product capabilities.
